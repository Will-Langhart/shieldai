Is the Bible historically reliable?

Gentle mode · 4 messages
⋯
W
Is the Bible historically reliable? My friend says it's just myths.
🛡
Great question. The Bible's historical reliability is actually well-supported by multiple lines of evidence.

Archaeological confirmation: Over 25,000 sites mentioned in the Bible have been confirmed by archaeology, including the Pool of Siloam (John 9:7) and the Tel Dan Stele mentioning "the house of David."

Manuscript evidence: The New Testament has over 5,800 Greek manuscripts — far more than any other ancient work. Homer's Iliad has only ~650.
W
What about contradictions people point to?
🛡
Most alleged contradictions dissolve under careful reading. Many are: differences in perspective between authors, figures of speech interpreted literally, or copyist variations in non-essential details.

Scholar Gleason Archer examined hundreds of supposed contradictions in his Encyclopedia of Bible Difficulties and found resolutions for each one.
